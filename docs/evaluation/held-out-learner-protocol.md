# Held-out learner pilot protocol

This is a preregistered-style pilot plan, not a result. It keeps learning-outcome evidence separate from the synthetic simulator and the model-judged tutor evaluations.

## Question

After a short PocketTA study session using a course's uploaded material, do learners answer a parallel set of course questions more accurately than they did before the session?

## Participants and safeguards

- Recruit at least 8 volunteer learners who have not authored or tested the selected questions. Treat the study as exploratory; do not claim statistical significance from this sample.
- Explain the activity, data collected, retention period, and withdrawal option before starting. Obtain consent and use random participant IDs; do not collect names, emails, grades, or other identifiers in the results sheet.
- Use one course topic set with learner-appropriate material and two reviewed, parallel 8-item forms. Review both forms for correctness and comparable difficulty before collecting responses.
- Keep answer keys and scoring criteria unavailable to participants until their post-test is complete. Do not provide the held-out form to the app's quiz-generation or model-configuration process.

## Procedure

1. Assign each participant a random ID and counterbalance which form is used for pre-test versus post-test.
2. Give the participant 8 minutes for the pre-test without PocketTA, notes, or outside help.
3. Give a fixed 20-minute PocketTA session using the same uploaded source and a short scripted set of learner tasks. Do not coach participants on answers.
4. Give the alternate 8-item form for 8 minutes without PocketTA, notes, or outside help.
5. Record item-level correctness, form order, actual study duration, and whether the participant completed each phase. Record technical issues without identifying details.
6. Optionally invite participants to a 7-day delayed test using a third parallel form. Report this separately and only if enough participants complete it.

## Analysis and reporting

Score each form as percent correct using the prespecified key. Report participant count, completion count, pre/post means and medians, per-participant score changes, and the mean/median paired change with the range. Report every exclusion and missing value. Break out form-order groups to reveal obvious order effects. Do not infer causation from this uncontrolled short pilot, and do not call a score increase a durable learning gain.

Report usability or satisfaction separately from correctness. Keep the raw sheet access-limited, retain only deidentified data, and delete it on the date stated in the consent information. The included CSV is empty so no result is implied before participants complete the protocol.
